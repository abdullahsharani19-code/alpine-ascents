QUESTIONS

Before You Head Uphill.

Start here for common questions about experience, equipment, preparation and choosing a program.

Do I need previous climbing experience?

Not always. Introductory courses can begin with fundamentals. More technical or high-altitude objectives generally require specific prior skills and preparation.

What equipment do I need?

Equipment depends on the program. Participants receive a program-specific gear list, and guides can help explain what is essential, optional or available to rent.

How do I choose the right expedition?

Consider experience, technical skills, fitness, altitude exposure, time available and the type of terrain you want to learn in.

Is mountaineering dangerous?

Yes. Mountains involve objective hazards and conditions can change rapidly. Training, experienced leadership, conservative decision-making and appropriate equipment are important parts of risk management.
